07How the model works

Raw Zillow data to an IRR, in one workbook

  1. Data & ETL

    Power Query loads Zillow’s national ZHVI and ZORI CSVs, filters to the Dallas–Fort Worth–Arlington metro and unpivots the monthly columns into long tables. ZIP codes stay as text.

  2. Submarket screen

    254 ZIPs have values, 220 have rents and 166 have the 3-year history needed to rank them on yield (70%) and rent growth (30%).

  3. Amortization

    A 360-month schedule on the $163,317 loan pays down to $0; the Year-10 balance is the loan payoff at sale.

  4. 10-year pro forma

    Gross rent → vacancy → EGI → five escalating expense lines → NOI → debt service → cash flow, all driven by named inputs.

  5. Exit and returns

    A terminal cap rate on forward NOI, net of 7% selling costs and the loan payoff, feeds levered and unlevered IRR, equity multiple, cash-on-cash and DSCR.

  6. Stress testing

    Two live Data Tables (exit cap × rent growth, price × mortgage rate) and a Goal Seek for breakeven rent.
